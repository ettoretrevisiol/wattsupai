# Training Goals

_Updated 23 Sep 2026_

## Current goal(s)

- **Hold or increase FTP before winter** — prevent the typical autumn/winter FTP fade that comes from dropping intensity after the outdoor season
- Keep VO2max above 60 heading into the indoor training block (current: 61, peak: 62)

## Priorities right now

- Add structured threshold work (Tue/Thu intervals) to complement the Z3/Z4 already happening on Saturday climbs
- Consistency over intensity — the base is solid (CTL 800+), the gap is mid-week quality stimulus
- Avoid overtraining — HRV and recovery signals are good, keep them that way

## Autumn terrain shift (Oct onwards)

- Moving from mountain climbs (30–60 min, Dolomites/Lecco/Friuli) to hilly terrain (15–20 min climbs)
- Target areas: Oltrepò Pavese, Varese hills, Brianza, other hilly zones around Milano
- Shorter climbs = higher intensity possible → treat each 15–20 min ascent as a natural threshold/VO2max interval
- Chain multiple climbs per ride to replicate the total quality load of a mountain day

## Schedule

- **Tuesday** — quality weekday (threshold/tempo intervals on flat or small hills, ~75-90 min)
- **Thursday** — quality weekday (threshold/VO2max, ~75 min)
- **Saturday** — long + hard (hilly ride, 3-4h, multiple 15-20 min climbs pushed hard)
- **Sunday** — long + easy Z2 (2.5-3h, strict cap ≤145bpm)

## Constraints / things to know

- Outdoor riding strongly preferred when weather allows
- Regular routes: Abbiategrasso (flat base), Oltrepò hills, Varese hills, Brianza climbs
- Based in Milano area
- Mountain days (Ghisallino, Lecco, Dolomites) less likely Oct–Nov due to weather/conditions
